# Privacy Policy - Grouped Indicator Table

Grouped Indicator Table is a Power BI custom visual.

- The visual does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or telemetry.
- All data rendered by the visual comes from your Power BI report and never leaves the Power BI environment.
- The visual makes no network requests and declares no privileges (`"privileges": []` in its capabilities), so it cannot access external services, local storage, or the clipboard.
- No cookies or tracking technologies are used.

For questions about this policy, open an issue at
<https://github.com/easthersteven/powerbi-visual-grouped-indicator-table/issues>
or contact <easthersteven@gmail.com>.
